1. beef-pickle.jpg     -> Authentic Kerala Beef Pickle
2. chicken-pickle.jpg  -> Spicy Kerala Chicken Pickle
3. fish-pickle.jpg     -> Coastal Seer Fish Pickle

Recommended format: JPG, PNG, or WebP.
Recommended aspect ratio: Square 1:1 (e.g. 800x800px or 1000x1000px).
