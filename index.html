At ZOOQE, we celebrate the rich culinary heritage of Kerala through uncompromising quality, authentic traditional recipes, and modern luxury presentation. We craft small-batch traditional pickles (achar) and source the finest single-origin Malabar spices.

Each jar is prepared with hand-selected ingredients, roasted Malabar spices, and cold-pressed gingelly oil, sealed in airtight glass jars to lock in the true essence and freshness of Kerala.
